Our Story

About Coffee House

At Coffee House in Colombo, Sri Lanka, we pride ourselves on being the go-to destination for coffee lovers and conversation seekers alike. Founded in 2010, we have dedicated ourselves to delivering an exceptional coffee experience in a cozy, inviting atmosphere.

We source our beans directly from the finest estates in Sri Lanka, Ethiopia, and Colombia — ensuring every cup tells the story of its origin.
